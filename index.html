A conversation already within reach

A simple pitch to centre management

Mel already has an established relationship with the shopping-centre Managing Director and has recently negotiated her own new shop lease. That creates a natural opportunity to open a commercial conversation around both the hoarding service and the advertising concept.

Suggested conversation:

“I’ve recently acquired a commercial Titan internal hoarding system and I’m looking at making it available for shop fit-outs and refurbishment work. I’d love to talk with you about whether there is an opportunity to provide the system here when hoarding is required, and what the centre would need from us in terms of approval, installation and compliance.

The other part I’m particularly interested in is the customer-facing space. Rather than having a blank wall during a fit-out, there may be an opportunity, with the centre’s approval, to use selected panels for centre promotions, incoming tenants, local businesses, events or community advertising. It potentially creates value from a space that would otherwise just be blank. I’d love to explore what could work for the centre.”

The aim is simply to open the conversation and understand what commercial and approval model could work for that centre.

The costs grow too
